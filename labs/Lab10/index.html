Gas Box Controls:
🔬 HOW TO EXPLORE THERMAL EQUILIBRIUM:
  • Select the "Thermodynamic Gas Box" from the scene menu.
  • Observe: The simulation starts in a non-equilibrium state with fast, light Hot Gas (Red) isolated from slow, massive Cold Gas (Blue).
  • Watch how the colors shift over time as collisions transfer kinetic energy, relaxing into a uniform **equilibrium temperature**.
  • Use the injection buttons to disrupt equilibrium and watch the system thermalize again!
